Standardized Diagnostic Protocols

Psychological Assessment & Diagnostic Testing

Standardized psychometric batteries administered by qualified clinical psychologists. Our evaluations provide formal differential diagnoses, cognitive baselines, school accommodations, and therapeutic roadmaps.

Pediatric & Youth

Neurodevelopmental Testing

Comprehensive diagnostic batteries for children and adolescents experiencing learning, attention, or social communication differences.

  • ADHD & Attention (Conners, Vanderbilt, Brown ADD)
  • Autism Spectrum (ISAA, CARS diagnostic criteria)
  • Dyslexia & LD (Reading, writing & math processing)
  • IQ Assessment (BKT, Malin's / WISC adaptation)
Adult Diagnostics

Psychodiagnostic & Personality

Detailed diagnostic testing for clarifying complex symptoms, personality structures, chronic mood disorders, and trauma.

  • Personality Dynamics (MCMI clinical multiaxial, 16PF)
  • Mood & Bipolar (Affective disorder differential indices)
  • OCD & Anxiety (Y-BOCS, GAD clinical scoring)
  • Complex Trauma (PCL-5, trauma impact scales)
Cognitive Functions

Neuropsychological Screening

Objective measurement of memory, executive control, working memory, attention flexibility, and cognitive retention.

  • Working Memory (PGI Memory Scale, auditory recall)
  • Mental Flexibility (Stroop test, trail-making tasks)
  • Post-Concussion Baseline (Cognitive sequelae recovery)
  • Geriatric Memory (MoCA, HMSE cognitive screening)
Aptitude & Career

Aptitude & Vocational Profiling

Psychometrically validated guidance for school stream selection, college majors, vocational fitness, and early-career alignment.

  • Differential Aptitude (DAT) (Spatial, mechanical, verbal)
  • Occupational Interest (Holland RIASEC career profile)
  • Study Habits Diagnosis (Academic fatigue & motivation)
  • Comprehensive Report (Actionable career pathway plan)
